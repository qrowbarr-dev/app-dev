## Breaking Bad

![Breaking Bad Poster](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th/id/OIP.0gjLZYa1IB9hAvMeUn0sVAHaLe?r=0&pid=ImgDet&w=201&h=311&c=7&o=7&rm=3)

**Breaking Bad** is an American neo-Western crime drama series created by Vince Gilligan for [AMC television](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AMC_(TV_channel)). It premiered on January 20, 2008, and ended on September 29, 2013.

### Premise
The story follows Walter White, a mild-mannered 50-year-old high school chemistry teacher in ABQ, New Mexico, who was diagnosed with stage three lung cancer. With the need to secure his family's financial security and his own legacy, Walter White decides to enter the methamphetamine trade, partnering with his former student Jesse Pinkman using a makeshift rolling meth lab. Their operations would eventually lead them to produce a highly pure form in the form of blue methamphetamine, becoming widely sought after within the drug world. Walter White would adopt a criminal pseudonym, going by the alias "Heisenberg", to hide his identity. But as he pulls himself deeper into the criminal underworld, and Heisenberg becomes of great renown, he would gradually come into conflict with his own family, particularly his brother-in-law Hank Schrader, a special agent in the Drug Enforcement Administration (DEA), as well as local cartels and gangs. 

The series is ultimately a story about a man's gradual moral decay. As the story progresses, Walter's goals of merely securing his family's financial future after his passing has become one of ambition to rise in the criminal world.
